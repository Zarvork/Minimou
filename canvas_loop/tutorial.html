Drawing App Tutorial

Drawing
Click and drag on the white canvas to draw. The brush follows your mouse smoothly.

Brush size
Use the slider in the top-left corner to change the size of your brush.

Colors
Select a color from the buttons on the right side of the screen.

Color Picker
Use the Color button to choose any custom color.

Eraser
Click the eraser button to draw with white.

Brushes
Circle uses the normal drawing brush. Baptiste uses your image as a circular brush.

Undo / Redo
Undo removes your most recent stroke. Redo restores a stroke that was undone.

Challenge
Choose Easy, Medium or Hard to launch a challenge. The challenges are currently placeholders.

Clear
Click Clear to erase the entire canvas. You will be asked to confirm first.
